The problems

parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date with both parts at 12 or below comes out with day and month swapped (“7 April went out on 4 July”); every tenant and every channel is affected. This is the fix #4471 has queued.

hint — booking-v3’s form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, so people type month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports through the form at tenants on booking-v3 (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour). The symptom is the same swap, so subject lines merge it into #4471, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint is wrong, the parser is not involved.

one-day-move — the date moves by one day, not a swap. The desk already noted these (desk-notes.json): “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”, “Every booking in June is one day off”, an invite that arrived before the booking was made. A day boundary between time zones, not day/month order. Neither the parser fix nor the hint fix touches it.

Why they ended up in one ticket

Merging by subject line (decision 0001) puts every “wrong date” report in one place, whatever the cause. The table on #4471 already shows the split: it marks hint as “not this ticket — no ticket of its own” and footnotes the one-day moves under parser. Nobody filed them.

What the desk does now

  1. Keep #4471 for parser only.
  2. Route new form reports from booking-v3 tenants to the hint ticket.
  3. Route reports where the date moved by one day (no swap) to the one-day-move ticket.
