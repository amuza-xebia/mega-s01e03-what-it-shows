The problems

parser — the shared parser read every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date with both parts at 12 or below came out with day and month swapped (“7 April went out on 4 July”), at every tenant and through every channel. Fixed with #4471.

hint — booking-v3’s form told people to type the month first. Its date field printed MM/DD/YYYY, so people typed month first, and the form read the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports through the form at tenants on booking-v3 (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour). The symptom is the same swap, so subject lines merged it into #4471, but the parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the form is retired.

One-day moves — the date moves by one day, not a swap. The desk already noted these (desk-notes.json) and counts them under parser by tenant: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”, “Every booking in June is one day off”, an invite that arrived before the booking was made. That reads as a day boundary between time zones, which neither fix changes, so it has its own ticket, still open.

Why they ended up in one ticket

Merging by subject line (decision 0001) puts every “wrong date” report in one place, whatever the cause. The cause table on #4471 already showed the split: it marked hint as “not this ticket — no ticket of its own” and footnoted the one-day moves under parser. Nobody filed them.

What the desk does now

  1. New swapped-date reports after 5 October are not #4471: check the tenant and form first.
  2. Reports where the date moved by one day go to the one-day-move ticket.
  3. When a big ticket’s cause table shows more than one cause, file one ticket per cause.
